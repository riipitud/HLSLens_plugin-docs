Getting started

Quick start

From a fresh install to a working setup in three steps.

1. Add the volume

Search for Advanced Post Process Volume in the Place Actors panel and drag it into your level.

It works like the engine's own Post Process Volume. You can move it, resize it, stack several and let them overlap. Tick Infinite Extent (Unbound) in the Details panel if it should affect the whole level, which is what most people want for their main volume.

Your level needs an Advanced Post Process Volume

Without one, the custom effects have nothing to read their settings from, and the scene can look distorted or oversaturated.

A new volume comes ready to use. It already has the Classic Prime lens flare profile and a material called M_EnginePPDisabler in its Blendables list. It's part of how HLSLens takes over from the engine's post-processing, so leave it there.

2. Turn on the effects you want

Everything is set in the volume's Details panel. Setup is the same in singleplayer and multiplayer projects.

Bloom, lens flares, halation and a light vignette are on from the start. Film Grain, Chromatic Aberration and Lens Distortion are off (intensity 0.0), so raise their intensity to turn them on.

0.0 means off, anything else means on

At exactly 0.0 an effect doesn't run and costs nothing. Even a tiny value like 0.05 has the full cost of the effect, so set unused effects back to 0.0 rather than just very low.

3. Turn HLSLens on or off at runtime

You can switch the plugin off and on without restarting the editor, which is handy for before/after comparisons:

Command What it does
r.HLSLens.Enable 0 Turns HLSLens off. Your volumes stay in the level.
r.HLSLens.Enable 1 Turns it back on.

Hide the volumes too

When you turn HLSLens off this way, also hide or disable your Advanced Post Process Volumes. The engine's post-processing wasn't made for HLSLens' settings, and running both can make the scene look darker than it should.

More commands are under Console variables. From here, the Lens flares and Bloom pages are good places to continue.
