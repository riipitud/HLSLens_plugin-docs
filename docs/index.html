Getting started

Overview

HLSLens replaces parts of Unreal Engine 5's post-processing with custom made effects, written in HLSL. It adds its own volume, the Advanced Post Process Volume and every setting lives in that volume's Details panel. So it works like the engine's Post Process Volume. The plugin doesn't delete or modify any engine files.

If you're new, start with Installation and then the Quick start.

Before you start

A volume is required

Without an Advanced Post Process Volume in your level, the custom effects have nothing to read their settings from.

  • Most optional effects are off by default. Film Grain, Chromatic Aberration and Lens Distortion start at intensity 0.0.
  • The Perceptual tonemapper changes your look. It shows emissive colors more accurately, so you'll likely need to adjust some light colors. ACES is still there and works with every HLSLens effect.
  • HLSLens costs slightly more GPU time than the engine's default post-processing. The Performance page has the numbers.
  • Motion blur, depth of field, anti-aliasing, global illumination and reflections aren't touched. They work the same as with the engine's post-processing.

Effects

Lens flares Ghosts, halo, streak and three kinds of glare, saved in reusable lens profiles. Seven presets included. Bloom A Kawase-blur bloom built to avoid the rings the engine's bloom can show in its falloff. Halation The red-orange film glow around the brightest lights. Chromatic aberration Basic and Spectral fringing, anamorphic stretch and depth masking. Film grain Procedural or texture-based grain with separate strength for shadows, midtones and highlights. Lens distortion Barrel and pincushion distortion that can be used together. Vignette and damage indicators A colorable vignette, and a directional damage indicator driven from Blueprints.

Color and exposure

Tonemapper ACES, or the Perceptual tonemapper that keeps lights closer to their real color. Selective control over 12 colors. LUT blending Two full color grades blended by distance, a mask or the time of day. Local exposure The engine's local exposure plus HLSLens' own method and highlight rescue.
